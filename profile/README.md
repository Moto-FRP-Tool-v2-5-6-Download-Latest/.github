## 🔹 What Is Moto FRP Tool?

Moto FRP Tool is a PC-based utility focused on servicing compatible Motorola smartphones. 📱⚙️ It communicates with supported devices through USB and can provide technical functions for device maintenance and authorized repair.

FRP stands for **Factory Reset Protection**. It is an Android security mechanism designed to prevent unauthorized use of a phone after a factory reset. Google explains that a protected device may require the owner's Google Account or screen lock after an unauthorized reset.

For legitimate owners, recovering the previously associated Google Account is the safest approach when a device becomes FRP-locked.
